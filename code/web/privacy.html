Privacy Policy

Last updated: December 15, 2025

Introduction

Ruwt ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application and related services (collectively, the "Service").

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. By using the Service, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Account Information: When you create an account, we collect your email address, display name, and authentication credentials.
  • Messages: We process the messages you send through our Runners (AI mediators) to provide our core service. Messages are processed transiently to suggest rewrites and facilitate delivery.
  • Usage Data: We collect information about how you interact with the Service, including features used, time spent, and interaction logs.
  • Device Information: We collect device type, operating system version, IP address, and unique device identifiers (Device IDs) for analytics, security, and troubleshooting.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the Service
  • Process your messages through our AI Runners
  • Authenticate your identity and prevent fraud
  • Send you updates, security alerts, and support messages
  • Analyze usage patterns to improve user experience
  • Comply with legal obligations

AI Processing & Third-Party Services

Our Service uses artificial intelligence (AI) to mediate messages. By using Ruwt, you acknowledge that:

  • Your message content is processed by our AI (specifically Google Gemini) to generate rewrite suggestions.
  • Message content is not used by us or our AI partners to train their foundational AI models.
  • Processed messages are not permanently retained on our servers after successful delivery to the recipient.

We may also use third-party services (such as Firebase or Google Analytics) to monitor infrastructure stability and analyze app performance. These services may collect pseudo-anonymized data including device identifiers and crash logs.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with:

  • Service Providers: Third parties that help us operate the Service (hosting, analytics, AI processing).
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law, subpoena, or to protect our rights and safety.
  • Business Transfers: In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets.

Data Retention and Security

Encryption: All data transmitted between your device and our servers, including message content, is encrypted in transit using industry-standard protocols (HTTPS/TLS).

Retention: We retain account information as long as your account is active. Message content is ephemeral and deleted from our servers shortly after processing and delivery.

Account Deletion

You have the right to delete your account and associated data at any time.

  • In-App: You can delete your account directly within the Ruwt app settings.
  • Web Request: If you cannot access the app, you may request full account deletion by emailing privacy@ruwt.social with the subject line "Account Deletion Request."

Upon deletion, all personally identifiable information associated with your account will be permanently removed from our active databases within 30 days. Some data may be retained in backups for a limited period or as required by law.

Children's Privacy

The Service is not intended for children under 13 years of age (or older, depending on your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we discover we have collected information from a child without parental consent, we will delete that information immediately.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at:

  • Email: privacy@ruwt.social
