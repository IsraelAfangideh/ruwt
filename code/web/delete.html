Delete Your Data

Request deletion of your Ruwt account and associated data

How to Delete Your Data

You have the right to delete your Ruwt account and all associated data at any time. There are two ways to request deletion:

Option 1: In-App Deletion

If you have access to the Ruwt app:

  1. Open the Ruwt app
  2. Go to Settings
  3. Select "Delete Account"
  4. Confirm your request

Option 2: Web Request

If you cannot access the app, submit a deletion request using the form below:

What Gets Deleted

When you request account deletion, we will permanently remove:

  • Your account information (email, display name)
  • Your message history
  • Your preferences and settings
  • Any data associated with your user ID

Processing Time: Deletion requests are processed within 30 days. You will receive an email confirmation once your data has been deleted.

Data We May Retain

In some cases, we may retain certain data as required by law or for legitimate business purposes:

  • Transaction records (if applicable) for tax/legal compliance
  • Anonymized, aggregated analytics data
  • Data in encrypted backups (automatically purged within 90 days)

Questions?

If you have questions about data deletion, contact us at privacy@ruwt.social.
