Spaces for Connection

Not all communication needs mediation

Cities

Communities of shared interest. Humans who know each other's identity and can contact each other directly—no Runner required.

  • Public existence, visible size
  • Open entry (unless restricted)
  • Direct 1-on-1 messaging

Closes

Private, invite-only spaces that function like digital homes. Maximum 100 members. Their existence cannot be confirmed by outsiders.

  • Completely private
  • Owner-controlled invitations
  • No AI mediation or surveillance

Inside Cities and Closes, humans connect directly—the way we always have—without AI interference or platform surveillance.
