We need to remember the original goal of the app as being a place where people can send messages to each other. 

"In the physical world we have the concept of a courier or messenger, but we do not really have the same concept in the virtual world. A courier spreads a message throughout a wide space but from human to human in a personalized way. Ruwt therefore aims to bring this way of human interaction to the digital world. It is DM only but you don't interact with the sender directly only with the runner. The sender and receiver do not see each other but the runner sees them both. The runner can reveal the identity of the sender and receiver to each other if they both wish but otherwise only the runner knows the sender and the receiver." - Spec 11_25_25

To support this we need users to be able to create an identity and log in. We need them to have an inbox and be able to receive messages inside it. We need to flesh out the messaging experience. What it looks like to get a message from a courier who received it and modified it from someone you do not know, who also does not know you. And also why would you even send a message to someone you don't know. How do two people who do not know each other get connected? These are things we need to think about, pray about, and iron out. 

Right now our app has peacemaker who modifies your mesages. We have a send button but we don't actually send it. In the future we will change send to go and the message will be broadcast at the runner's discresion. And then two users could choose to connect to each other. In the future we will truly honor the spirit soul and written body of the original divine specification.
