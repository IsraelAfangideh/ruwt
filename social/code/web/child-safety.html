Child Safety and Exploitation Policy

Last updated: December 29, 2025

Our Commitment

Ruwt has a zero-tolerance policy for child sexual abuse material (CSAM), child sexual exploitation (CSE), and any content that sexualizes or endangers minors. We are committed to protecting children and will take immediate action against any user who violates this policy.

Prohibited Content and Behavior

The following content and behaviors are strictly prohibited on Ruwt:

  • Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM): Any visual or written content depicting sexual abuse or exploitation of minors (individuals under 18 years of age).
  • Sexualization of Minors: Content that sexualizes, objectifies, or inappropriately targets minors, including but not limited to:
    • Sexualized images, descriptions, or discussions involving minors
    • Grooming behaviors or attempts to establish inappropriate relationships with minors
    • Content that encourages or facilitates sexual contact with minors
  • Child Exploitation: Any content or behavior that exploits, endangers, or harms minors, including:
    • Trafficking or recruitment of minors for sexual purposes
    • Sharing or soliciting personal information of minors for inappropriate purposes
    • Any form of child endangerment or abuse

Age Restrictions

Ruwt is not intended for users under 13 years of age (or the minimum age required in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we discover that we have collected information from a child without parental consent, we will delete that information immediately and terminate the associated account.

Reporting Mechanisms

If you encounter content or behavior on Ruwt that violates this policy, please report it immediately through one of the following channels:

  • In-App Reporting: Use the reporting feature within the Ruwt app to flag inappropriate content or users.
  • Email: Report directly to safety@ruwt.social with the subject line "CSAE Report"
  • Emergency Situations: If you believe a child is in immediate danger, contact your local law enforcement authorities immediately.

All reports are reviewed promptly, typically within 24 hours. We take all reports seriously and investigate them thoroughly.

Our Response to Violations

When we identify or receive reports of CSAM, CSE, or other child safety violations:

  • Immediate Action: We will immediately remove the violating content and suspend or permanently ban the associated account(s).
  • Law Enforcement Cooperation: We will report all instances of CSAM and child exploitation to the appropriate law enforcement authorities, including:
    • National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) in the United States
    • Relevant authorities in other jurisdictions as applicable
  • Preservation of Evidence: We preserve all relevant information and content to assist law enforcement investigations.
  • No Appeals: Violations of this policy result in permanent account termination with no opportunity for appeal.

Content Moderation

We employ a combination of automated systems and human review to detect and prevent child safety violations:

  • Automated Detection: We use industry-standard tools and technologies to identify potential CSAM and exploitation content.
  • Human Review: All flagged content is reviewed by trained moderators who understand the severity of child safety violations.
  • User Reporting: We rely on our community to report suspicious behavior and content.

Cooperation with Law Enforcement

Ruwt fully cooperates with law enforcement agencies worldwide in investigations related to child sexual abuse and exploitation. We:

  • Respond promptly to valid legal requests and subpoenas
  • Preserve and provide relevant information as required by law
  • Report suspected CSAM to appropriate authorities without requiring a court order
  • Work with international law enforcement agencies to combat child exploitation

Prevention Measures

To prevent child safety violations, we:

  • Verify user age during account creation
  • Monitor user behavior and content for suspicious patterns
  • Implement restrictions on interactions between adults and minors
  • Provide clear reporting mechanisms and educational resources
  • Regularly update our detection and moderation systems

Resources for Victims and Families

If you or someone you know has been a victim of child sexual abuse or exploitation, please seek help from:

  • National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC): www.missingkids.org or call 1-800-THE-LOST (1-800-843-5678)
  • Childhelp National Child Abuse Hotline: 1-800-4-A-CHILD (1-800-422-4453)
  • RAINN (Rape, Abuse & Incest National Network): www.rainn.org or call 1-800-656-HOPE
  • Your Local Law Enforcement: Contact your local police department or emergency services (911 in the United States)

Contact Us

For questions about this policy or to report child safety concerns, please contact us at:

  • Email: safety@ruwt.social
  • For urgent child safety matters, please contact your local law enforcement immediately

Policy Updates

We may update this Child Safety and Exploitation Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technology, or legal requirements. We will notify users of any material changes by posting the updated policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.
