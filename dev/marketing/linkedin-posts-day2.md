## Post 4: Observation / Thought Leadership (no hard sell)

I've been noticing something while building ruwt.dev.

The developers who are best at using AI aren't the ones who write the longest prompts. They're the ones who know when NOT to ask the AI.

They read the error message first. They check the docs. They use a cheap model for simple tasks and only reach for the expensive one when it matters.

AI coding efficiency isn't about prompting. It's about judgment.
