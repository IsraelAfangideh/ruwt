### Learnings
- Twitter cold DMs require Premium — not worth it at current stage. Use public replies instead.
- LinkedIn connection request notes (300 chars) are the best free cold outreach channel.
- Warm contacts in existing LinkedIn messages convert way faster than cold — prioritize those.
- Posts from small accounts get limited reach (~50-200 people). DMs are 70% of the conversion engine, posts are 30%.
- Simple + clear hooks outperform technical/specific ones. Post 1 (829 impressions) vs Post 2 (147 impressions).
- Personalized DMs matching existing rapport convert way better than templates. Israel's own voice > Claude's templates.
- "Early adopter" / "badge of honor" / "one of our first users" is an effective incentive for builders.
